8-Ball Pool game

- Click, drag, and release to launch the cue ball.

- Hitting in the cue ball will halve your score.

- The goal is to hit all balls of one type into the pockets. The 8-ball must be hit in last.

- Hitting the wrong type of ball into the pocket will cost you points.

- Hitting in the 8-ball before you finish hitting in all of your own ball type will cause you to instantly lose.

Start by hitting the cue ball.

Select a ball type

- Once you choose a type, your goal is to hit all balls of that type into the pockets. The 8-ball must be hit in last.

- Hitting the wrong type of ball into the pocket will cost you points

- Hitting in the 8-ball before you finish hitting in all of your own ball type will cause you to instantly lose.

(These are not the official rules of 8-ball pool. I just made these up because this is singleplayer.)
